/ Common questions

Questions about using the Tyre Guide

How do I find the right tyre for my machine in the Tyre Guide?

Type the machine model into the search box, for example CAT 992K or Komatsu WA600, and the guide returns the records whose typical fitment includes that machine. You can then narrow by brand, TRA service code or rim diameter. In our view that gets you to a shortlist fast, but it is a shortlist: confirm size, load index, speed symbol and inflation pressure against the current manufacturer databook and the OEM fitment data for your machine before ordering.

Which brands and TRA service codes does the Tyre Guide cover?

The published sample covers 27 records across 14 brands: Bridgestone, Michelin, Goodyear, Yokohama, Continental, BKT, Aeolus, Techking, Triangle, Double Coin, Galaxy, Alliance, Mitas and MRF. TRA service codes in the sample are E-4, L-3, L-4, L-5 and G-2, on rim diameters of 24, 25, 45, 49, 51, 57 and 63 inch. The full database of 600+ records is in production. Code meanings are set out in the TRA service code matrix.

Are the specifications in the Tyre Guide guaranteed?

No. Specifications and fitments are drawn from manufacturers' published databooks and may be superseded by a later edition. They are general reference, not engineering or fitment advice, and no fitment or suitability is guaranteed. Confirm fitment, load and pressure against current manufacturer and OEM data for your specific machine before ordering or fitting.

How does a Tyre Guide record differ from a manufacturer databook?

A databook is one manufacturer's own catalogue, published as a PDF and not searchable against anyone else's. A Tyre Guide record carries the same kind of published data (brand, pattern, size, TRA code, construction, compound, typical fitment) but sits in one cross-brand index you can search by size, code or machine. The databook remains the authority on the numbers; this guide is the way to compare across brands, plus our own read on where each pattern earns its place.
