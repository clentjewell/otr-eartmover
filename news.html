Manufacturer

Tier-one makers signal radial supply easing into H2

Lead times on large radial sizes (49″-63″) are reported to be shortening as capacity that was redirected during the 2024-25 squeeze comes back online. Practical takeaway for buyers: less reason to over-order, but hold a sensible safety stock on the sizes you can't run without.

Curated · manufacturer bulletins + trade desk
Compliance

AS4457 handling reminders circulating ahead of audits

Sites are refreshing storage, inspection and mixing-rule documentation. If you mix radial and bias on a machine, or run mismatched brands on an axle, make sure the paperwork matches the primary standard, AS4457:2019, the reference matrix is on this site.

Curated · AS4457 summary · see Reference
Market

Pilbara cycle changes nudging TKPH requirements up

Longer hauls on a couple of operations are pushing required TKPH higher than the fitted tyre was specified for. Worth re-running the numbers on the TKPH calculator before the next changeout, heat, not tread, is usually what ends these tyres.
