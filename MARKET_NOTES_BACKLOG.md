# Market Notes — source backlog (content pipeline)

Citable source material for future **Market Notes**, gathered by web research. Each
row is a potential note. Build them with the pattern in `MARKET_NOTES_TEMPLATE.md`
— **take the figures, attribute them, write everything else ourselves.**

**Status key:** ⭐ build first · ✅ figures page-verified & freely visible ·
⚠ unverified / blocked / dated — open by hand before citing.

**Blanket caveat for market-research (Category 1):** these are marketing-driven
estimates with undisclosed methodology and differing market definitions. Base-year
"market size" spans roughly **USD 19bn–34bn** across publishers. Always cite a
single figure as *"one estimate among several,"* paired with its base year and
forecast window. (This is exactly the "forecasts disagree" angle a note can use.)

## Category 3 — OEM / tier-one manufacturer data

**Dominant theme for a note:** giant-OTR/mining supply is **consolidating into Yokohama**
(Goodyear OTR + Trelleborg + Alliance), while **Bridgestone and Michelin defend via US
capacity** and Australian service acquisitions. A clean structural-supply-side story.
Treat every figure tagged ⚠ excerpt as unverified until read on the corporate page.

## Category 4 — Mining-industry demand signals (tyre-consumption proxies)

*Proxy logic: each ultra-class rigid haul truck (Cat 793 / Komatsu 930E, ~220–290 t)
runs **6 giant OTR tyres**, lasting ~4,000–8,000 hrs on hard Pilbara/coal haul roads.
So fleet counts and production tonnages are direct replacement-demand proxies. These
are the strongest Australia-specific, tier-1-sourced hooks in the backlog.*

### 6. Rio Tinto — Q4/FY2024 Production Results ⭐ ✅ (tier-1 primary)
- **URL:** https://www.riotinto.com/en/news/releases/2025/rio-tinto-releases-fourth-quarter-production-results
- **Published:** 16 Jan 2025
- **Public figures:** Pilbara iron ore 2024 production **328.0 Mt** (100% basis, −1% YoY); shipments **328.6 Mt**; Gudai-Darri at **50 Mtpa** run-rate; medium-term objective **345–360 Mtpa**.
- **Note angle:** Australia's largest single haulage task — sustaining ~328 Mt/yr = a continuously running rigid-truck fleet and steady giant-OTR replacement; the 345–360 Mtpa ambition signals fleet expansion.
- **Reliability:** Tier-1 primary company data.

### 7. BHP — FY2024 Operational Review (SEC Form 6-K) ✅ (tier-1 primary)
- **URL:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/811809/000119312524188292/d871158d6k.htm
- **Published:** Jul 2024 (FY ended 30 Jun 2024)
- **Public figures:** Total iron ore FY2024 **260 Mt** (record); WAIO **255 Mt** (BHP share) / **287 Mt** (100% basis); South Flank ramped to **80 Mtpa**; medium-term WAIO target **>305 Mtpa**.
- **Note angle:** WAIO's ~287 Mt task runs a heavily autonomous Cat 793 fleet; >305 Mtpa target implies more trucks and rising OTR consumption.
- **Reliability:** Tier-1 primary (SEC filing, no paywall).

### 8. Fortescue — FY2024 shipments + Liebherr fleet-electrification deal ⭐ ✅
- **URLs:** shipments https://gmk.center/en/news/fortescue-shipped-191-6-million-tons-of-ore-in-fy2023-2024/ · fleet deal https://www.miningweekly.com/article/partnering-for-first-electric-autonomous-operations-2024-08-16
- **Dates:** Jul 2024 (shipments) · 16 Aug 2024 (fleet deal)
- **Public figures:** FY2024 shipments **191.6 Mt** (record); existing **200+** diesel autonomous haul trucks; **US$2.8 bn** Liebherr deal = **475 machines**, incl. **360 autonomous battery-electric haul trucks** replacing ~two-thirds of the fleet.
- **Note angle:** ~360 new trucks × 6 = **~2,160 OTR tyre positions** being renewed this decade — a dateable, quantified fleet-replacement wave feeding aftermarket demand.
- **Reliability:** Shipments tier-1 (company, via tier-2 outlet); fleet numbers tier-1 announcement via tier-2 press.

### 9. Australia coal production & met-coal exports — Resources & Energy Quarterly ✅ (tier-1 government)
- **URLs:** https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/resources-and-energy-quarterly-december-2024 · corroboration https://www.mining-technology.com/analyst-comment/australia-coal-production-2024/
- **Published:** Dec 2024
- **Public figures:** Coal production 2024 ~**550 Mt** (+2.8%), thermal >60% of output; metallurgical coal exports **151 Mt (2023–24)** rising to **174 Mt by 2025–26**; thermal export earnings **A$37 bn (2023–24)** easing to **A$29 bn (2025–26)**.
- **Note angle:** The ~550 Mt/yr coal task (Bowen Basin, Hunter Valley) is the second national OTR-consuming pool after iron ore.
- **Reliability:** Tier-1 federal government for volumes/earnings; ~550 Mt rounding is tier-2.

### 10. Rio Tinto autonomous haul-truck fleet ⚠ (dated — context only)
- **URLs:** https://www.mining-technology.com/news/rio-tinto-expand-autonomous-truck-fleet-pilbara-australia/ (2017) · https://www.mining.com/rio-tinto-autonomous-trucks-now-hauling-quarter-pilbara-material/ (403-blocked, snippet only)
- **Public figures:** ~**433 trucks** total Pilbara fleet (2017 basis; recent snippet "almost 400"); "**130+ autonomous ≈ 30%**" (2017 target); recent snippet ~20% autonomous hauling ~a quarter of material; retrofits Komatsu 930E (29) + Cat 793 (19).
- **Note angle:** A ~400-truck Pilbara fleet ≈ **~2,400 giant-OTR positions** in 24/7 duty — the densest OTR replacement pool in Australia.
- **Reliability:** Tier-2 trade press; 2017 figure stale — verify current count before publishing.

### 11. BHP WAIO autonomous & haul-truck fleet detail ✅ (tier-2)
- **URL:** https://im-mining.com/2022/10/03/bhp-opens-up-on-waios-autonomous-and-electric-fleet-future/
- **Published:** 3 Oct 2022
- **Public figures:** WAIO fleet **220 haul trucks**; autonomous target **~190 trucks / ~85%**; autonomous fleet 89% availability, haulage costs 20% below WAIO average.
- **Note angle:** 220 ultra-class trucks ≈ **1,320 giant-OTR positions** at WAIO alone; autonomy raises utilisation → faster tyre wear/replacement cadence.
- **Reliability:** Tier-2 press quoting BHP; 2022, slightly dated.
