- **OTR / Earthmover** — loaders, graders, dump trucks, scrapers, compactors

- **Agricultural** — tractors, harvesters, combine, sprayers, trailers

- **Construction** — backhoe loaders, telehandlers, cranes, compact equipment

## TYRE INSPECTION PROCESS

Jewell Tyres uses a formal Tyre Inspection Report for all site inspections.

**Site/Job Header fields:**
Date, Time, Site, Location, Reporting on site, Safety PPE required, Special safety considerations, Communication on site (Phone / Radio Channel / Other), Transport on site, Any other notes

**Per-tyre inspection fields (one row per tyre):**
Lot No | Tyre Size | Brand | Ply/Star Rating | Pattern | Compound | Tread Type | Tread Depth | Tyre Description | Repairs/Damage | Crown | Sidewall (Inside) | Sidewall (Outside) | Bead | Liner | Serial No | Pic No | Price | Comments

**Report footer:** Pre-filled with Jewell Tyres business details and QR code linking to the digital report.
