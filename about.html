/ Premise

Why does this site exist?

SECTION 01
PREMISE

Most of what's published about OTR tyres lives inside manufacturer databooks. Bridgestone has theirs. Michelin has theirs. Goodyear, Yokohama, Continental, BKT, Aeolus, Techking, Triangle, every brand publishes their own.

Each one is manufacturer-aligned. Each one is behind a PDF download. None of them are searchable across brands. None of them are cross-referenced. None of them tell you what the tyre next door actually does in the same application.

That gap is why this site exists. We've spent five decades collating manufacturer material to do our own trading job, matching tyres to machines, machines to applications, applications to conditions. The working knowledge that lives in our spreadsheets, our heads, and our supplier relationships.

This site is that working knowledge, made available. Free. Searchable. Cross-brand. Independent. The reference we wish had existed when we started, and the reference we think the industry needs now.

/ Independence

No manufacturer paid for any of this.

OTR Earthmover Tyre is editorially independent. No manufacturer sponsors the site. No manufacturer reviews the content before it's published. No manufacturer gets preferential placement in the Tyre Guide, the Knowledge Portal, or the Market Notes.

When the Portal says Brand X is the better fitment for a machine in a particular application, that's the call, not what a sponsor wanted said. When a piece in Market Notes says a tier-one premium isn't worth it for a secondary fleet, that's the call.

The only commercial tie on this site is to Jewell Tyres, and that tie is openly endorsed, not hidden. Jewell maintains the site. Jewell is the trader you'd talk to if you wanted to buy or sell any of the tyres referenced here. The endorsement is the point: independence from manufacturers, anchored to a 50-year independent trading business.

/ Common questions

Questions about who runs this site

Who publishes OTR Earthmover Tyre?

Jewell Tyres publishes OTR Earthmover Tyre. Jewell Tyres is a family-owned, independent off-the-road tyre trading business founded by David Jewell in 1974 and based in Wodonga, Victoria, trading across Australia and New Zealand.

Does any tyre manufacturer pay for or approve this content?

No. No manufacturer sponsors the site, reviews content before publication, or receives preferential placement in the Tyre Guide, the Knowledge Portal or the Market Notes. The site carries no advertising and is free to use.

Where does the technical data on this site come from?

Six sources: manufacturer databooks across 19 brands, the Tire and Rim Association Year Book, AS4457:2019, OEM fitment guides from 7 machine makers, Tyre Stewardship Australia material on disposal, and 50 years of Jewell Tyres trading experience. Specification values are reference that may be superseded by a later databook edition, so confirm them against current manufacturer and OEM data before acting.

What does independent mean when the publisher also sells tyres?

Independent here means independent of every tyre manufacturer, not disinterested. Jewell Tyres holds no manufacturer alignment, distributorship or sponsorship, and trades all 19 brands in the directory. It also trades the tyres referenced on this site commercially, and says so on every page rather than hiding it. Read the evaluative content as the opinion of a trader with skin in the game.

How do I report a mistake or a missing tyre?

Send it through the contact page. Wrong specifications, missing patterns, out-of-date figures and tyres that should be in the Tyre Guide are all worth telling us about. Corrections are how the reference improves, and we would rather be told than be wrong.
