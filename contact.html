/ Good to know

Common questions

A few things worth knowing before you get in touch.

Is this where I buy tyres?

No, buying and selling are handled by Jewell Tyres at jewelltyres.com.au. OTR Earthmover Tyre is the free technical reference; the trading happens over there.

Does it cost anything to use, or to be listed?

No. The reference is free and ad-free, and adding or correcting a tyre record costs nothing. We don't take placement fees from manufacturers, the site isn't aligned to any of them.

How fast will I hear back?

Most messages get a reply within two business days. Corrections are usually actioned faster than that. If it's time-critical buying or selling, call David direct on 0419 358 439, that beats the form.

How do you verify a correction?

Technical fixes are checked against the manufacturer databook or the relevant standard (TRA service codes, AS4457) before a record changes. If you can include a source with your note, it goes through faster.

Can I reuse the data?

It's a public reference, quote it and cite OTR Earthmover Tyre. For bulk or commercial reuse (feeds, datasets, redistribution), get in touch first so we can keep it accurate and attributed.

Who maintains the site?

Jewell Tyres, Australia and New Zealand's independent specialists in big tyres since 1974. Independent, ad-free, and not aligned to any manufacturer. More about the site →
