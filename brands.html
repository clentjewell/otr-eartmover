/ Common questions

Questions about OTR tyre brands

How many OTR earthmover tyre brands are in this directory?

Nineteen. Five are classified tier 1 (Bridgestone, Michelin, Goodyear, Yokohama, Continental), ten tier 2 (BKT, Aeolus, Techking, Triangle, Double Coin, Galaxy, Alliance, Mitas, MRF, Solideal / Camso) and four tier 3 (Titan, Trelleborg, Firestone, Yokohama OTR). Each entry lists country of origin, year founded, OTR focus and notable patterns.

What do tier 1, tier 2 and tier 3 mean on this page?

They are OTR Earthmover Tyre's own editorial classification, based on Jewell Tyres' independent trading experience in the Australian and New Zealand market. Tier 1 covers the established global premium makers, tier 2 credible mid-tier makers, tier 3 specialty and niche positions. It reflects our view of typical market positioning and supply, not an official industry standard, and manufacturers may hold a different view.

Does the tier ranking say anything about a tyre's safety or compliance?

No. The tier is about market positioning and supply, not safety, compliance or fitness for a particular application. A tyre's suitability for a machine and duty depends on size, load index, speed symbol, TRA service code, TKPH rating and compound, confirmed against current manufacturer and OEM data and AS4457:2019.

Are mid-tier OTR tyre brands worth buying?

In our view, often yes, but it depends on the application. Several tier 2 patterns have earned their place in Australian civil, quarry and secondary mining fleets at a materially lower delivered cost. We'd lean to tier 1 where cut and heat conditions are at their hardest and downtime is the dominant cost, and to tier 2 where conditions are moderate and delivered price matters. That is trader opinion, not engineering advice, and it is worth testing per application on your own site data.

Which countries do OTR earthmover tyre brands come from?

In this directory: Japan, France, Germany and the USA for the tier-one makers, then China, India, the Czech Republic, Sri Lanka and Sweden across the mid-tier and specialty brands. Several brands sit inside larger groups, so corporate ownership and country of manufacture can differ from the badge. Ownership changes over time, so verify current details with the manufacturer.
