26.5R25, complete reference

The most common L-3, L-4 and L-5 loader tyre in Australian and New Zealand mining and civil construction. 25-inch rim, radial construction, broad brand and pattern coverage.

/ Overview

A defining loader size.

26.5R25 is the standard fitment for the CAT 980-988 series, Komatsu WA470-WA500, Volvo L220-L260, and Liebherr L 580, the workhorse mid-large wheel loader class on Australian mining and civil sites. The size is consistent across all major OEM machine generations from the late 1990s to current.

The 26.5R25 tyre fits a 25-inch rim. The "26.5" is the section width in inches (the widest point of the inflated tyre). The "R" indicates radial construction. Bias-construction 26.5-25 exists but is increasingly rare, radial dominates current fitment.

Across the major manufacturers, 26.5R25 is offered in L-3 (general purpose), L-4 (deeper tread, longer life), L-5 (extra-deep tread, cut and heat resistance), and L-5S (smooth, underground). The right TRA code depends on the application, L-3 for civil work, L-4 for general mining, L-5 for primary production loaders in hard-rock and abrasive conditions, L-5S for underground LHDs.
