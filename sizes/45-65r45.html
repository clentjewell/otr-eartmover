45/65R45, complete reference

The primary fitment for the CAT 992K and Komatsu WA800 class wheel loader. Large-frame L-5 tyre, narrow supply base, premium pricing, premium service life.

/ Overview

The large wheel loader workhorse.

45/65R45 is the standard fitment for the CAT 992K and Komatsu WA800 class wheel loader, the production-scale machines that move ore at the digger face on Pilbara iron ore, gold and copper operations. The size is large, heavy, and demanding to manufacture; supply base is narrower than mid-size loader tyres like 26.5R25.

The naming convention reads: section width 45 inches, aspect ratio 65 (section height is 65% of section width), radial construction, 45-inch rim. The tyre is roughly 2.7 metres tall and weighs over 3 tonnes per unit.

Almost all 45/65R45 production runs L-5 specification, extra-deep tread, heat-resistant or cut-resistant compounds. The application is severe enough that L-4 spec wouldn't carry the duty. Compound choice (heat-resistant vs cut-resistant) depends on the cycle, long cycles in hot ambient favour heat-resistant, shorter cycles in abrasive material favour cut-resistant.

Through 2024-25 this size experienced the most acute global supply tightness in the OTR market. Lead times stretched to 25+ weeks for non-contract buyers. The market normalised through 2026 with capacity additions from all major manufacturers, but 45/65R45 remains a size where supply discipline matters.

Common machines

Caterpillar 992K, 992L · Komatsu WA800-3, WA800-8 · Hitachi ZW550 (some variants) · Letourneau L-1350 (legacy fitment via adapter)

Tier-one patterns available

  • Bridgestone VRDP (heat-resistant L-5), long-running default for Pilbara iron ore
  • Michelin XLD D2 L-5, premium alternative
  • Goodyear RL-5L, tier-one alternative
  • Yokohama RL5K+, strong residual value, often better delivered price

Mid-tier patterns available

  • BKT Earthmax SR468, Indian-manufactured L-5, significant cost gap to tier-one
  • Aeolus AL57, Chinese-manufactured, wider trader-channel availability
  • Techking ETOT-L5, specialist mid-tier, increasingly common in secondary fleet
/ Before you act General reference only, not engineering or fitment advice. Figures are indicative for a typical configuration and can be superseded between editions. Confirm load, pressure, fitment, TKPH, repair, scrap and mixing decisions against current manufacturer data, AS4457:2019 and a competent person for your specific tyre, rim and machine before acting.

Note: in our trading experience, for tier-one mining contracts on primary production fleet, the cost premium for tier-one in 45/65R45 is generally justified by supply continuity, retreadable casing residual value, and the lower downtime risk. We'd generally keep mid-tier to secondary fleet positions rather than the digger face on a primary 992, reasonable operators weigh this differently.
