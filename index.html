/ Common questions

Questions people ask about this site

What is OTR Earthmover Tyre?

OTR Earthmover Tyre is a free technical reference site for off-the-road earthmover and mining tyres, published by Jewell Tyres, an independent Australian OTR tyre trader operating since 1974. It covers 19 tyre brands, TRA service codes, tyre naming, mixing rules under AS4457:2019, a 40-term glossary, common failure modes and a TKPH calculator.

Is this OTR tyre reference free to use?

Yes. The site is free, ad-free and needs no login. That covers the Tyre Guide, the TRA service code matrix, the glossary, the failure-mode reference, the AS4457:2019 summary and the TKPH calculator.

Who maintains the site, and is it independent of tyre manufacturers?

Jewell Tyres of Wodonga, Victoria maintains the site, and has traded off-the-road tyres since 1974. No tyre manufacturer sponsors the site or reviews content before it is published. Jewell Tyres trades these tyres commercially, so independent here means independent of every manufacturer, not disinterested.

Where should I start if I need a tyre for a specific machine?

Start in the Tyre Guide and search on the machine model, which returns the sizes, patterns and TRA service codes commonly fitted to it. In our view the machine search gets you to a shortlist quickly, but the shortlist is a starting point: confirm size, load index, speed symbol and pressure against the current manufacturer databook and the OEM fitment data for your machine before ordering.

Is anything on this site engineering or procurement advice?

No. Specifications here are general reference drawn from published databooks and standards, and may be superseded by a later edition. Everything evaluative is the opinion of an independent trader, not a tyre engineer. Confirm fitment, load, pressure and any safety-critical decision against current manufacturer data, AS4457:2019 and a competent person before acting.
